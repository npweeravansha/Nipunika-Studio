06 START A PROJECT

Not sure what to prepare before starting your website? Here's a simple guide to help you get everything ready.

What I need from you.

You don't need to have everything ready before contacting me. Just bring your idea — these details will help me understand your project and create the right website for you.

01

PROJECT IDEA

Tell me what the website is for. For example, a business, personal brand, portfolio, service or a new idea.

02

CONTENT

Send your text, business information, services, about section, contact details and any other content you want on the website.

03

LOGO & IMAGES

Your logo, product photos, team photos, banners or any other images you'd like to use on the website.

04

DESIGN REFERENCES

Send websites, screenshots, Figma designs or other examples that show the style and look you like.

05

PAGES & FEATURES

Tell me which pages you need such as Home, About, Services and Contact, plus any special features you want.

06

CONTACT & SOCIALS

Your WhatsApp, email, Instagram, Facebook and other social links you want displayed on the website.

07

DOMAIN & HOSTING

If you already have a domain or hosting, let me know. If you don't have them yet, I can guide you on what you need.

08

YOUR GOAL

Tell me what you want the website to achieve — get more clients, showcase your work, promote your business or build your brand.

DON'T HAVE EVERYTHING READY?

That's completely fine. Just tell me your idea and I'll help you figure out what you need.

08 FAQ

Questions, answered.

A few things clients usually want to know before starting a project.

01 How long does a website take? +

A simple landing page can usually be completed within a few days. Larger projects may take longer depending on the number of pages, content and interactions required.

02 Do you design and develop the website? +

Yes. Nipunika Studio can handle both the visual direction and frontend development using HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

03 Can you make the website mobile responsive? +

Absolutely. Every website is designed to work across desktop, tablet and mobile screen sizes.

04 Do you provide hosting and domains? +

Hosting and domain registration are not included in the standard packages. They can be discussed separately if needed.

05 How do I start a project? +

Simply send a message through WhatsApp or email with a short description of your project. We can then discuss the requirements and next steps.
